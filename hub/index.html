Prototype 1

ShopList: Smart Lists for Smart Shoppers

Live prototype ShopList shopback-shoplist.vercel.app View the prototype

1. The Problem

Shoppers plan purchases outside ShopBack on spreadsheets, Google Docs, and Post-its. When newer ShopBack users open the app, overwhelming store discounts dilute purchase intent. ShopBack misses the moment when buying decisions are made and customers end up not getting cashback.

  • Overwhelming Offers

    in-app promos and store offers are organized by store rather than by what the shopper needs, so the context of what to buy is missing.

  • Planning Happens Elsewhere

    shoppers plan in notes, docs and screenshots, spend hours comparing prices and still miss Cashback they could have earned.

  • Discovery Belongs to Social

    on mobile, ecommerce’s fastest-growing channel, Instagram and TikTok own impulse discovery. ShopBack’s opportunity is planned purchases, where Cashback compounds when ShopBack starts the journey.

2. The Solution

ShopList is a smart shopping list that helps users plan, track and save. This builds the habit of opening Shopback and providing them context layer when they are ready to make a purchase.

  • Plan

    users add items by text, voice, photo or browser extension into Buy Now, Wish List or Recurring.

  • Track

    an agent built on the Shopping Assistant monitors prices and reviews, and sends daily or weekly digests on price drops and deals.

  • Save

    for every item, ShopList finds the best price after Cashback, vouchers and shipping.

Hurdles at Scale

  • Product Recognition

    This is a significant technical hurdle that can be mitigated by restricting products to specific sub-categories before gradually expanding product catalog. Matching natural-language entries to the right SKUs, stores and brands is hard; draw on existing global SKU catalogs and use follow-up prompts to clarify intent.

  • List Flexibility

    users may not adopt the Buy Now, Wish List and Recurring taxonomy; roll out custom lists (gifts, home vs office, seasonal).

  • Regional Retail Habits

    in markets where shoppers buy offline, link lists to in-store offers.

  • AI Cost at Scale

    recurring price reports require multiple tool calls; price each SKU once for all watchers, script the Cashback calculation and default to weekly digests.

3. The Synergy

ShopList extends the Shopping Assistant from a blank chat to the user’s own list, surfacing product offers instead of brand discount rates.

  • Users

    the best products at the best bottom-line price, with less time spent researching.

  • ShopBack

    first-party purchase intent and the habit of starting online shopping in ShopBack.

  • Brands

    incremental orders from new and more frequent customers.

4. Measuring Success

Funnel stageMetric
  1. 1
    Awareness

    Users who see ShopList in-app or are aware of feature

  2. 2
    Engagement

    Users adding items, opting into daily or weekly price reports, opening price reports

  3. 3
    Usage

    Weekly active list users; items per user, number of users with populated list

  4. 4
    Purchase conversion

    ShopList items bought via ShopBack

  5. 5
    Repeat conversion

    Buyers who buy from their list again within 90 days

Prototype 2

ShopKeeper: A First-Party Game to Drive Everyday Engagement

Live prototype ShopKeeper shopback-game.vercel.app View the prototype

1. The Problem

ShopBack is top of mind only when users are ready to buy, and the time between purchases goes to apps that increasingly sell as well.

  • Inconsistent Engagement

    app use spikes around purchases and promo events; between them, ShopBack is rarely top of mind.

  • Competing App Time

    The war for attention and screen share is on. Users spend their free time in mobile games and social apps such as Instagram and TikTok.

  • Shopping and Games Are Converging

    Social apps (TikTok, Instagram, Pinduoduo) now have native shopping, while LinkedIn, Shopee, Netflix and Discord have added in-platform games.

2. The Solution

ShopKeeper is a lightweight first-party game inside the ShopBack app that gives users a reason to return every day.

  • Game Core Loop

    Combines adventuring, loot collection and a store simulator.

  • Daily Loop

    five-minute sessions built around a daily dungeon and a promo calendar for selling loot. Activating a real ShopBack deal triggers a “Campaign Rush” of in-game customers.

  • Bridge to Partners

    Playing daily unlocks real Cashback deals; later, real-world sales events can become in-game events.

Hurdles at Scale

  • Live Ops and Content

    players need a steady content roadmap; per-market calendars with AI-assisted localization keep events relevant.

  • Release Speed

    build in HTML5 so content updates ship without app releases.

  • Proving Impact

    pilot in one market with a holdout group to test the lift in transactions and GMV; engagement may vary by cohort and region.

3. The Synergy

ShopKeeper gives users a daily reason to open ShopBack, alongside the Shopping Assistant and ShopList.

  • Users Retention

    a daily habit that surfaces deals they would otherwise miss.

  • ShopBack App Time

    more time in the app, with a potential lift in total purchases.

  • Engage with Brands

    once the player base is large enough, sponsored products and interstitial ads become a new channel to test.

4. Measuring Success

Funnel stageMetric
  1. 1
    Engagement

    Time spent in the ShopBack app and in ShopKeeper

  2. 2
    Frequency

    Lift in weekly sessions, players vs non-players

  3. 3
    Conversion

    Deals activated from the game; transactions and GMV vs holdout
